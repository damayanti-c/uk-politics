# Midlands regeneration funding: headline

Over the last decade, Westminster governments spent £19 billion on funding regeneration, of which £3.85 billion went to regions in the Midlands - 20.2% of the total, despite the fact the Midlands constitutes 16.2% of the UK and 19.2% of the English population.

Within the Midlands, the West Midlands took £2.02 billion (10.6% of the UK total, 52.6% of Midlands funding) and the East Midlands £1.83 billion (9.6% of the UK total, 47.4% of Midlands funding). The East Midlands holds 45.0% of the Midlands population and the West Midlands 55.0%, so per head the East Midlands is the slightly more heavily funded half.
